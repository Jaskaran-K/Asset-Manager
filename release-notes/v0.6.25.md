## What's new in Asset Manager 0.6.25

### UI Overhaul
Every window, viewer and dialog has a quieter, more modern look. The bars hold fewer controls, buttons have no borders until you hover them, and tools show up only when they apply. Nothing has been removed, and every shortcut is the same.

**The main window**
- **One slim top bar:** the sidebar button, where you are (profile / Library), a centred search pill with AI Search and Search by Colour inside it, then More, the view switch and Settings.
- **More:** Open Folder, Refresh and the library tools.
- **The selection bar:** select files and a bar floats up over the grid with Preview, Tag, Favorite, Send to Artbook and Find Similar. In a narrow window it shows icons only.
- **Soft selection:** selected rows and cards get a soft accent tint everywhere, not a solid block.

**Viewers**
- **A slim header:** the file's name, type and size, then Info, More, Full Screen and Close. The footer is gone.
- **Floating tools:** the tools float at the bottom of the picture and fade while the mouse is still. **Info** opens the details as a floating card.
- **3D models:** shading, material and lighting are borderless dropdowns in one floating pill, with Render beside them. Roughness, HDRI exposure and the alpha settings appear in a small pill above it, only while they apply.
- **Material and Render windows:** the tools float over the view, and the settings sit in a side panel with one main button. In the Material window that's **Send to Maya**.
- **PDFs:** one slim bar with a centred Find pill. The marking tools float at the bottom of the pages and stay put.
- **Videos:** a slim bar under the picture. Jump to Time and the 720p proxy are in More.

**Artbook**
- **Bars:** the top bar is borderless. The note, drawing and group bars and the Images list float over the board.
- **A bar for the selected picture:** its most-used actions, without going through the right-click menus.

**Settings and dialogs**
- **Settings:** every page has an icon, and settings are shown as cards: what each one does on the left, its control on the right. Every page scrolls when the window is small.
- **One layout for every dialog:** what it does at the top, the choices under small headings, two or three options as a switch, and a bar at the bottom with Cancel and the main button at the right.

**Polish**
- **Motion:** hovering a button, a row or a card fades it in, and buttons spring back when clicked. **Settings > Appearance > Animations** turns this off.
- **Keyboard:** Tab goes through a window in reading order. Whatever you've reached shows an accent ring, which goes at your next click. The arrow keys move the ring through the grid.
- **Sand:** a new warm, light theme with an amber accent.
- **Pale accents:** filled buttons and ticks get dark text when the theme's accent is too pale for white. This covers your own Theme Studio themes too.
- **Switches:** the chosen option stands out clearly, and the other options are readable in every theme, Neon Cyan included.

### Fixes
- **Align:** in the Artbook, Align lines pictures up edge to edge instead of overlapping them.
- **Note sizes:** the note bar's text size list is wide enough to show its sizes.
- **Storage:** Settings > Storage no longer squashes its buttons in a small window.
- **Material window:** it can't be made so small that the 3D view disappears.
- **Render window:** the LuxCore note is no longer cut off in a short window.
