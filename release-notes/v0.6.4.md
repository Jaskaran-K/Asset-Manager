## What's new in Asset Manager 0.6.4

### Alphas
Grey height images (ZBrush alphas, displacement and height maps) are now recognised by themselves: any grey image whose name doesn't say it's another kind of grey map (AO, gloss, roughness, metal, mask...).

- **A lit relief in the grid.** Alphas are drawn as a clay-grey relief lit from the top left, as ZBrush shows them, so you can see their shape at a glance instead of a flat grey card. It's drawn from their full 16-bit heights: at thumbnail size that keeps thousands of levels where 8 bits keep about 230, which showed as steps on gentle slopes. It costs nothing extra: about 15 ms per thumbnail, the same as before. **Settings > Appearance > Alpha thumbnails** switches between **Relief** and **Normal** (the flat grey image).
- **`is:alpha`** in the search finds them (`-is:alpha`: everything else), inside archives too.
- **3D in the viewer** (**3**): an alpha as a surface with the 3D viewer's materials, lights and keys. A **Height** slider, **Invert** (**I**) for alphas made the other way round, and **512 px** or **1024 px** detail (about 2 million triangles). Even a 760 MB displacement PSD opens in 3D in about a tenth of a second.
- **Tile** (**T**) shows any image repeated **2×2** or **3×3**, edge to edge, to check its seams.

The first time you start this version, your images' details are read once more in the background to find the alphas: about a minute for 2,800 images. The app works as usual meanwhile.

### ZBrush and other app files
Brushes, tools, projects, Painter projects, Maya, 3ds Max and Toolbag scenes and PureRef boards are now in the library, on disk and inside archives, under **ZBrush** and **App Files** in the sidebar. `.psb` images are in too.

- **Each card shows the icon of the app it opens in**, read from the app on your computer, unless there's a sure picture: a picture beside the file named after it or named like a preview, or the render every `.blend` holds. Cards take under a millisecond each with an icon, and read only the start of the file for a `.blend` or a board.
- **Open In** (right-click) opens a file in ZBrush, Blender, Maya, Photoshop, Substance 3D Painter or Marmoset Toolbag, whichever fit it. OBJ and FBX are imported into Blender and Maya. Files inside archives are unpacked first.
- **Settings > Apps**: the apps are found by themselves wherever they're installed, the newest version first. Pick another version, or browse to one, there. **File Types...** changes what an app is offered for.
- **Add App...** puts any other program in Open In, such as Krita: pick it, name it, give it its file types. Its icon comes along, and a new type like `.kra` joins the library.
- **Import into the window you have open.** Press **Install Helper** for Maya or Blender in Settings > Apps, and from the next start OBJ and FBX go into the window that's open instead of starting another. Scenes still open in a new window. The helper only ever imports model files, and **Remove Helper** takes it out.
- **Double-click** a brush, project or scene to see its picture and details, with a button (or **O**) that opens it. Nothing starts until you ask.
- **PureRef boards** show the picture they hold of themselves. Double-click opens one as an Artbook page, the same page every time, using a page you've already imported if there's one with its name. A changed board says so, and right-click > **Import Again** makes a new page: your edits are never overwritten.

### Fixed
- **A rare freeze when a scan ended.** If a scan finished at the very moment the window was doing certain work (creating a menu was enough), the app could stop responding for good. The scan's cleanup now happens where this can't occur.
