## What's new in Asset Manager 0.4.5

### Import your PureRef boards
- **Bring your layouts over:** the new **Import** button in the Pages sidebar (or dropping files on the board) turns PureRef boards (`.pur`) into Artbook pages, with every image where you left it: position, size, rotation, flips, crops, opacity, and notes. Pick several boards at once; each becomes its own page.
- Groups come in ungrouped (everything keeps its place), and drawings are left out for now. Boards saved by PureRef 1.x need opening and saving once in PureRef 2 first.

### Share pages and export pictures
- **Export as PNG** (**Ctrl+E**, or right-click > Canvas): the whole board, or just the selection, as one picture, as sharp as its images allow.
- **Share a page:** right-click a page > **Export Page...** saves it as one `.artbook` file with its images inside, ready to send. Open one with the Import button or by dropping it on the board.

### Arranging and adjusting images
- **Auto-Arrange (A)** lays the selection (or everything) out in neat rows, keeping their sizes. Right-click > Images also has **Align**, **Distribute** and **Match Size**.
- **Crop (C):** drag the handles, then Enter (or C, or click elsewhere) to apply. The whole image is kept, so a crop can be reset any time.
- **Grayscale:** **G** for the selected images, **Ctrl+G** for the whole board, to judge values.
- **Opacity:** a slider in the right-click menu.
- **Eight handles** around a selection: corners and sides scale from the opposite edge.
- **Reset** one change at a time: rotation, size, flips or crop (**R** resets all but the crop).
- **Colour Picker (I):** click anywhere on the board to copy that colour's hex code.

### Notes
- **T** adds a note where the cursor is. Notes follow the theme's colours.
- A **format bar** appears above the selected note: text colour, highlight, note colour, bold / italic / underline (**Ctrl+B / I / U** while typing), size, font, and alignment.
- **Custom colours:** a picker with a colour square, hue and opacity sliders, a screen dropper, and HEX / RGB / HSL. The note changes as you pick, and the whole pick is one undo step. It floats beside the note; drag its title to move it.
- Highlights have rounded corners, like the notes.

### The Artbook in its own window
- In **Separate View**, the **Window** button (also in the right-click menu) has **Always on Top**, **Opacity**, and **Click-Through**, so the board can sit over another app while you work in it.
- **Ctrl+Alt+T** turns click-through on and off from any app, and a tray icon shows while it's on.
- **Send to Artbook:** right-click images in the library to add them to any page, or a new one.

### Good to know
- The right-click menu on the board is now grouped: Canvas, Images (Selection, Arrange, Align, Match Size) and Window.
- Unsigned app: Windows SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info > Run anyway**.
