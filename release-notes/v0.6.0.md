## What's new in Asset Manager 0.6.0

This release is about speed and reliability: the whole app was reviewed and measured, part by part, before any new features.

### Faster
- **Library scans are 13 to 17 times faster.** Each folder is read once instead of asking the disk about every file.
- **Scrolling a big grid:** after a fast scroll, the cards you land on appear in about half a second (it took up to 13 seconds). Thumbnails you scrolled past are skipped.
- **Thumbnails use at most 150 MB of memory.** The rest reload from the disk cache.
- **Changing the theme** no longer reads every thumbnail again. It took about 3 seconds; now it's instant.
- **Big OBJ models open much faster:** a 270 MB OBJ in about 5 seconds instead of nearly a minute. Hard edges stay sharp, because the file's own normals are kept.
- **Big images open in the background,** so the app never waits for them:
  - a 400-megapixel TIF opens in about 2 seconds (it took 9);
  - a large PSD opens in about 1 second, with less than half the memory;
  - a 2 GB TIF that used to show blank now opens.
  - Images over 8192 pixels are shown at 8192, and the details list their real size.
- **Closing the viewer** stops a model that's still loading.

### New
- **HDR images:** `.exr` and `.hdr` files get thumbnails and open in the viewer. HDRIs are tone mapped so bright skies keep their detail.
- **Search:**
  - File name words in any order: `rock wall` finds `Wall_Rock_02.png`; `"rock wall"` keeps them together.
  - A minus excludes, as with tags: `-ext:png`, `-is:fav`.
  - Sizes without `<` or `>` match to the precision typed: `size:10mb` finds 9.5 to 10.5 MB.
- **Renaming a tag** updates the collections that use it.
- **Title bars follow the theme.** On Windows 11 they take the theme's own colour.

### Look and feel
- Dropdown arrows sit neatly inside their rounded box, and ticked checkboxes show a tick.
- Light Mode's grey text is easier to read.
- Long library paths are shortened in the middle (`C:\Users\…\Library`) instead of being cut off mid-letter.
- Thumbnail cards change colour as you preview themes in Settings.

### Your data is safer
- **Daily library backups:** your tags, favourites and ratings are backed up automatically once a day. The last 7 are kept, apart from the backups made before updates.
- **A damaged library database** is no longer a dead end. The app offers to restore the newest good backup, and keeps the damaged file in the backups folder.
- **Damaged files no longer lose your work.** Artbook pages, the Artbook's folders, PDF notes and the settings file each keep their previous save. If a file is ever damaged (a disk error, a sync tool), the app opens that copy instead.
- **A damaged Artbook page stays in the Pages list,** in its folder, instead of disappearing.
- **Leftover images:** pictures from items you deleted are cleared from an Artbook page's folder the next time it opens.
- **The library database keeps files on a drive that's offline,** and anything with tags, a favourite or a rating, in case the file turns up again.

### Fixes
- **Live updates** keep working on large libraries. After a rescan, most folders used to stop being watched, so changes went unnoticed until the next Refresh.
- **Fixed a crash** when a scan was replaced just as it started.
- **Settings is freed when it closes.** Each opening used to stay in memory, and theme switches got slower every time (after a few openings, 0.7 seconds instead of 0.05).
- **Show in Explorer** selects the file whatever its name. Names with commas, `%` or `&` used to trip it up.

### Good to know
- Unsigned app: Windows SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info > Run anyway**.
