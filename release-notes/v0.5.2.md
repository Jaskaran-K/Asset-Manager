## What's new in Asset Manager 0.5.2

### Big boards, fast and light
- Images load at the detail they're seen at. Zoomed out, each shows a small copy; the ones in view get sharper as you zoom in, all the way to the full picture (no longer capped at 4096 pixels). Sharper copies are let go once an image is well out of view.
- A board with 191 large images now holds about 13 MB of pictures instead of about 1.4 GB.
- Pictures load from the middle of the view outwards, and small copies are cached, so a page opens almost at once the second time. **Settings > Video > Clear Cache** clears these too.
- **Export as PNG** and **Copy** always use the full pictures, whatever is showing, and exporting no longer freezes the window while it prepares them.

### Choose where your pages are kept
- **Settings > General > Artbook** picks the folder for your pages, for example a synced OneDrive or Dropbox folder.
- **Change...** moves them there: everything is copied first and only then removed from the old folder, so nothing is lost if the copy fails. A folder that already has pages shows those instead. **Use Default** moves them back.
- If the folder isn't there when the app starts (an unplugged drive), the default one is used until it's back.

### Motion
- Arrange, Align, Distribute, Stack, Match Size, the resets, and undo / redo glide images to their new places.
- Pictures fade in as they load.
- In the asset grid, cards slide into their new rows when the window is resized.
- All of this follows the Motion setting in **Settings > Appearance**.

### Smaller improvements
- **Drawing:** a new **Smooth** button on the draw bar (on to start with) steadies lines drawn with a mouse, as with a pen.
- **Notes** start with centered text (Clear Formatting returns to it too).
- **Settings > Guide** has an Artbook guide and the Read Me, right in the app.

### Fixes
- Fixed an error ("QScrollBar already deleted") when scrolling some lists with the mouse wheel.
- Fixed a possible crash in the asset grid while the window was being resized.

### Good to know
- Unsigned app: Windows SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info > Run anyway**.
