## What's new in Asset Manager 0.3.0

### 📦 New installer (faster start)
- **AssetManager-Setup.exe** installs the app for your user account (no administrator prompt). It starts noticeably faster than the single-file exe, because nothing has to be unpacked at every launch.
- Updates from **Settings > About** run the new installer silently and restart the app.
- The portable **AssetManager.exe** is still available and still updates itself.
- Your settings and library are never touched by installing, updating or uninstalling.

### ⚡ Much faster with big libraries
- The grid only works on the cards you can see. With 50,000 files, refreshing went from about 12 seconds to a tenth of a second, and clearing a search from 7.5 seconds to instant.
- **Instant start:** your library appears straight away on launch while a fresh scan runs in the background.
- **Thumbnail cache:** thumbnails are saved on disk, so big textures are decoded once instead of every session.

### 🔄 The library keeps itself up to date
- Files you add, remove, rename or edit in your folders show up within a couple of seconds while the app is open. No more Refresh.
- **Moved files keep their tags.** Move or rename a tagged, rated or favorite file outside the app and it keeps everything.

### 🔍 Better search
- `-tag:WIP` finds files *without* a tag; `tag:hero|villain` finds files with *any* of them. Click a tag chip to switch it between "has" and "doesn't have".
- Search by details: `is:fav`, `rating:>=3`, `ext:png`, `size:>10mb`, `res:>=4k` (image or video resolution), `dur:<30s` (video length), `poly:<10k` (3D model faces). Hover over the search box for a reminder.
- **Collections:** click ☆ in the search box to save a search in the sidebar.

### ⭐ Favorites and ratings
- Select files and press **F** to star them, or **1** to **5** to rate them (**0** clears). Stars and ratings show on the thumbnails, and the sidebar has a **Favorites** row.

### 🧰 New Tools menu
- **Find Duplicates:** finds files with identical content and moves the copies you pick to the Recycle Bin. One copy of every file is always kept.
- **Back Up / Restore Library** and **Export / Import Tags** (tags, favorites, ratings and collections, for moving to another PC). Backups are also made automatically before upgrades and updates.
- **Undo / Redo** (**Ctrl+Z** / **Ctrl+Y**) for tag, favorite and rating changes. **Cancel** on Settings > Tags now really undoes the changes made there.

### 🖼️ Viewer and video
- **Drag files out** of the grid into Explorer, Blender, Photoshop, Unreal and more.
- In the viewer, **Page Up / Page Down** (or the ‹ › buttons) step through the files in the grid without closing it.
- Video: **playback speed** (0.25× to 3×, presets plus a fine slider), **mute** (click the speaker or press **M**), **frame step** (**,** and **.**), **A-B loop** (**L**), and videos **resume where you left off** (**Home** starts over).
- Smoother, animated buttons and menus. **Settings > Appearance > Animations** turns them off, or follows Windows.

### 🛠️ Fixes and reliability
- **Updating no longer freezes at "Installing 100%".** The update itself had finished; the app froze while showing "done". Downloads are now also checked against GitHub's checksum before anything is replaced.
- **Crash log:** crashes, freezes and errors are saved to a log. After a crash the app tells you on the next launch, and **Settings > About > Open Crash Log** opens it for bug reports.
- Libraries stored inside a folder named like `build` or `dist` are now scanned correctly.
- Every button has a tooltip, and there's a welcome screen on first launch.

### ⚠️ Good to know
- **Updating from 0.2.0:** 0.2.0's updater still has the freeze. If it gets stuck at 100%, close it and start Asset Manager again; the update is already installed. Updates after 0.3.0 don't have this problem.
- To switch from the portable exe to the faster installed version, download **AssetManager-Setup.exe** below.
- Unsigned app: Windows SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info > Run anyway**.
