## What's new in Asset Manager 0.7.50

### KeyDial, for the Huion KD100 (a bonus app)
**KeyDial** is a small app of its own for the **Huion Mini KeyDial KD100**, in place of Huion's software for it. Its 18 keys and dial do what you set, per app. It runs in the tray and starts with Windows.

**Getting it**
- The installer adds it to the Start menu as **KeyDial**, unless you untick its box.
- It's also on this page on its own, as **KeyDial.exe**.

**The editor**
- Your KD100 as it is, each key showing what it does. Click a key or the dial, or press it on the KD100 while KeyDial's window is in front.
- A key can press a **Shortcut** (click the box and press it), **Hold Keys** (Space to pan, Alt to orbit), **Type Text**, **Scroll** (with Ctrl, Shift or Alt held), **Open** a program, file or web page, run a **Macro**, change the **Dial Mode**, or switch **Profile**.
- **Macros:** several steps in a row. **Record** writes down what you type, with the pauses as waits.
- A key set to just Ctrl, Shift or Alt stays held while you hold it, so it works with your other KD100 keys (hold Ctrl, press A).
- Every change works straight away.

**Profiles**
- Starter layouts for **Maya** (first), ZBrush, Substance 3D Painter, Photoshop, Blender, Marmoset Toolbag, media players (**Multimedia**) and **Browsers**. **Other Program...** covers anything else.
- **Default** is a number pad, as the KD100 is laid out for one: the tall key is Enter, the wide one 0.
- **Auto mode** switches profile by the app in front. **Manual mode** uses the profile you pick in every app.
- **Also in every web browser:** one box makes a profile work in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Opera, Brave, Vivaldi and any other browser Windows has installed.

**The dial**
- Each profile has its own modes (Maya: Zoom, Timeline, Keyframes, Undo / Redo). Pressing the dial steps to the next one.
- A pop-up at the bottom of the screen says which profile and mode are on. It never takes the focus.

**The tray menu**
- **Pause**, **Auto** / **Manual Mode**, **Start with Windows**, **Run as Administrator** (only needed for apps that run as administrator), Show Pop-up and Quit.

### Good to know
- **The KD100's own shortcuts:** while KeyDial runs it takes over the KD100, and the KD100's built-in shortcuts stop until it's unplugged. That's why KeyDial stays in the tray.
- **Using the Huion driver for a tablet:** set each of the KD100's keys and its dial to **None** in the driver, or each press does two things.
- **Media keys in Opera GX:** if Play/Pause does nothing on YouTube there, try **Hardware Media Key Handling** at `opera://flags`.
