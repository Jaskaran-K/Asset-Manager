## What's new in Asset Manager 0.4.0

### The Artbook: reference boards beside your library
- **Pages** are endless boards for collecting reference images. Drop files on them, drag images in from the asset grid, drag pictures straight out of a web browser (Google Images results included), or paste with **Ctrl+V**.
- **Arrange freely:** drag to move, drag a corner to scale, the round handle to rotate (hold Shift for 15° steps). **H** / **V** flip, **]** / **[** bring to front / send to back, **R** resets, **Ctrl+Z** undoes.
- **Get around:** the mouse wheel zooms toward the cursor, middle-drag or Space+drag pans, **F** frames everything, and double-clicking an image zooms to it.
- **Saved automatically:** pages save a couple of seconds after each change, and when you switch page or quit. Images from your library are linked, with a small copy kept in the page in case the file moves.

### Pages sidebar
- The **menu button** at the top left of the Artbook (or **Ctrl+B** while you're working in it) slides out your pages, each with a small picture of its board.
- **Folders inside folders:** keep pages organized however you like. Drag pages and folders to reorder them, or drop one on a folder to move it inside.
- **New Page** and **New Folder** buttons at the top. The **pencil** on a page or folder (or **F2**) renames it, and right-click has Duplicate, Move To and Delete.
- **Search** filters your pages by name (or by folder name).
- Like the library's sidebar, it can stay **pinned** beside the board or **float** over it on frosted glass, with your images blurred behind it.

### Four views
- The **View** button at the top right switches between the **Asset View** and the **Artbook View**. Its arrow (or **Ctrl+1** to **Ctrl+4**) also offers:
  - **Split View:** your assets and the Artbook side by side. Drag the divider; the app remembers where.
  - **Separate View:** the Artbook in its own window, for a second screen. Closing it takes you back to the Asset View.
- When a side gets narrow, its toolbar buttons shrink to icons (hover over them for their names).
- The app reopens in the view you left it in.

### Good to know
- **Ctrl+B** shows or hides the sidebar of the side you're working in: the categories on the asset side, the pages in the Artbook.
- Deleted pages go to the **Recycle Bin**, so you can restore them from there. Deleting a folder never deletes its pages: they move up a level.
- Pages are stored in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\AssetManager\artbook`, next to your library database.
- Unsigned app: Windows SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info > Run anyway**.
