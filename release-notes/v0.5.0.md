## What's new in Asset Manager 0.5.0

### Groups
- **Ctrl+G** groups the selected images and notes. A soft panel shows behind the group, and clicking any member, or the panel's empty space, picks up the whole group, so it moves, arranges and aligns as one block.
- **Alt+click** picks one member on its own. **Ctrl+Shift+G** ungroups.
- A selected group gets a small bar above it:
  - **Colour:** the theme's, white, black, or **Custom...** with the live colour picker (opacity included).
  - **Lock:** the group can't be moved, resized, turned, flipped, cropped or deleted until it's unlocked.
- PureRef boards now import with their groups and group colours.

### Drawing
- **D** draws freehand strokes on the board. The bar at the top sets the pen's colour and size, and **E** turns on the eraser: drag over strokes to remove them. D again, Esc or Done stops.
- Strokes move, scale, rotate, group and undo like everything else.

### The image list and files
- **L** (or the new **Images** button) lists every image on the page: a small picture, its name, its size, and whether it's linked to a file, kept in the page, or missing its file. Click one to select and frame it.
- **Relink Missing Images...** finds images whose files moved: pick the folder they're in now and they're found by name.
- **Replace Image...** puts a new picture in place of an image, keeping its place, size, crop and adjustments.
- **Show in Explorer**, **Open Source File**, and **Copy > File Path** for images linked to files.
- Imported PureRef images keep their names, and link back to their original files when those are still on your computer.

### More for images
- **Caption...**: a line of text under an image that moves and scales with it.
- **Pixelated**: hard pixel edges when zoomed in, for pixel art and textures.
- **Stack** (under Align): piles the selection up like a hand of cards.

### Pages from the board
- Right-click > **Pages** makes a new page, or duplicates, renames or exports this one.

### Changed shortcuts
- **Ctrl+G** is now Group. The whole-board grayscale moved to **Shift+G** (**G** still turns grayscale on for the selected images).

### Good to know
- Unsigned app: Windows SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info > Run anyway**.
