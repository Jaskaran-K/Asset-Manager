## What's new in Asset Manager 0.6.10

### Earlier versions of Artbook pages
Each page used to keep only its previous save, so a mistake noticed the next day couldn't be undone. Now the first time a page changes on a day, how it was before is kept: the last 10 versions of each page.

- **Where:** right-click a page in the Pages sidebar (or the board > Pages) > **Earlier Versions...**. Each version is listed with a picture of the page, when it was, and how many images and notes it had.
- **Restore This Version** makes the page as it was then. How it is now is kept as a version first, so you can always go back.
- **Restore as a New Page** makes a copy beside it and leaves the page as it is.
- Versions are small (the images are shared, not copied). Images that only an earlier version uses are kept for as long as that version is.
- Duplicating or exporting a page leaves its versions behind.

### Settings > Storage
One page for everything the app keeps on disk, with the total:

- **Files unpacked from archives**, now kept up to **5 GB** by default (it was 20 GB). Choose 1 to 20 GB.
- **Thumbnails and preview copies**, and the **Artbook's small copies**: **Clear** them, and they're made again when needed.
- **LuxCore** and **Real-ESRGAN**: **Remove** them, and they download again if you render or upscale.
- **Library backups** and **your Artbook pages**: only opened from here, never cleared.

The cache sections on the Video and Archives pages moved here.

### Report a Problem
**Settings > About > Report a Problem...** makes a zip for a bug report: the crash and session logs, the app's and Windows' versions, your processor, memory and graphics card, and how big the library is (counts only, no names). Your settings go in only if you tick it, since they hold your folders' paths. **Nothing is uploaded.** **Open a GitHub Issue** opens the form to attach it to.

### Dropdowns
Dropdowns now open under their button instead of over it, show up to 10 items and scroll after that, so a long list (like the 3D viewer's Lighting) no longer runs off the window. They slide down into place, and the lines between groups of items are back.

### Also changed
An exported page (.artbook) no longer carries the originals of upscaled images: they stay with your copy of the page.
