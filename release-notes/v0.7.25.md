## What's new in Asset Manager 0.7.25

### Courses
**Course View** (**Ctrl+5**, or the View button's arrow) opens your courses in a window of their own. A course holds lesson videos and the scene files and references that go with them, in an order of your own, with your progress and notes.

**The Courses page**
- Each course has a card with a frame from its first lesson and how far you are.
- One button carries on where you left off (**Continue: 05 Head blockout**).
- If the window was open when you quit, it opens again with the app.

**Making a course**
- **Open Folder as Course...** drafts a course from a folder:
  - Lessons are ordered by the numbers in their names (`01_`, `Lesson 3`, `Part 2`).
  - Without numbers, they're ordered by the steps their names describe, in a 3D course's usual order (`ModelingAndSculpting` before `BakingAndTexturing`).
  - Folders of videos become chapters.
- **Files go with their lessons**, by number or by step: `05_Head.ztl`, or a `Bakes` or `Marmoset` folder. Documents go with the whole course, and anything else waits in a tray for you to drag onto its lesson.
- **Zipped courses are unzipped first**, each archive beside itself. Every file is then read back and checked against the archive.
  - A different file of the same name is kept, never overwritten.
  - Files keep their dates from the archive.
  - The archives are offered for the Recycle Bin only when every file checked out. RAR and 7z need 7-Zip.
- **New Course** starts an empty course: drag in videos and files from anywhere. Nothing is copied or moved, and **Relink...** finds files that moved.

**Watching**
- Lessons play in the app's own player.
- A lesson ticks itself done at 90% watched, and the next one starts by itself.
- Each lesson's files open in their app, Maya first, and pictures drag onto the Artbook.
- **N** notes the moment you're at. Notes show as dots on the time bar, and each one also goes on the course's Artbook page with the frame it was made on.

**Editing**
- Reorder, rename and group lessons into chapters, and drop files on a lesson.
- A long stream becomes lessons: **K** cuts the video at the moment playing, and each part is a lesson of its own.

**Lessons from the web**
- **Add Link...**, or drag a link in from your browser; the lesson takes the site's title.
- **YouTube** plays in the course window, through YouTube's own player. It resumes, ticks itself done, and supports notes with frames.
- **A link straight to a video file** plays in the app's player.
- **Anything else** opens in your browser. That includes Vimeo and paid course sites, and you tick those lessons done yourself.
- **Download for Offline** saves an HD copy with the course. It appears only if you've installed yt-dlp yourself (`winget install yt-dlp`). Downloading from YouTube is against YouTube's terms, so that's your call.

### Fixes
- **Video player:** its buttons follow the theme when you change it while a video is open. They used to vanish in Light.
- **Seek bar:** it can show marks (a course's notes) and a lesson's part of a long video.

### Good to know
- **Bigger download:** the app now includes Qt's built-in web browser, for YouTube lessons, so it's larger than before.
