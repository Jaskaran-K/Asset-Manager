## What's new in Asset Manager 0.5.5

### A new PDF viewer
- **Sharp at any zoom:** pages are drawn for the zoom you're at. Ctrl+wheel (or pinch) zooms toward the pointer, and the zoom box takes presets or a typed size. **W** fits the width, **F** the whole page, and **Ctrl+0** is actual size.
- **Getting around:**
  - **Left** / **Right** turn pages and **Home** / **End** jump to the first and last.
  - Type a page number in the page box.
  - Drag to move the pages.
  - Page Up / Page Down still switch files.
- **Sidebar (Ctrl+B):**
  - **Contents** lists the book's chapters.
  - **Pages** shows a thumbnail of every page.
  - **Notes** lists your marks.
  - Click any of them to go there. Links in the PDF work too.
- **Find (Ctrl+F):** every match is highlighted. Enter / F3 goes to the next and Shift+Enter / Shift+F3 to the previous. A 480-page book is searched in a fraction of a second.
- **Select and copy text** with the Select Text tool (**S**), across pages too. **Ctrl+C** copies.
- **Your marks:** **Highlight** (**H**), **Pen** (**D**), **Note** (**T**) and **Eraser** (**E**).
  - Each has its own colour, and the pen has a size.
  - With the Hand tool, click a mark to pick it. Drag a drawing or note to move it, or press Delete to remove it.
  - Undo and redo with Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y. **Marks** hides them all.
- **Send a page to the Artbook** (right-click a page), with or without your marks.
- **Export a copy** of the pages with your marks drawn in: one PDF or PNG images, at 150 or 300 dpi.
- **The PDF itself is never changed.** Your marks and the page you were on are kept by the app, matched to the file's contents, so they follow it when it's renamed or moved. A PDF reopens where you left it.

### Artbook
- **PureRef drawings now import:** each stroke keeps its colour and width, and a drawing's strokes move together.
- **A lighter active area** sits behind everything on a page. It grows and moves smoothly as you add or spread out images. Exports leave it out.

### Fixes
- Fixed a crash while switching themes and background textures quickly in Settings.
- Enter in a viewer's text box no longer also opens the tag editor.
- Checkboxes and option buttons now follow the theme. They used to show as pale pink circles.

### Good to know
- Unsigned app: Windows SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info > Run anyway**.
