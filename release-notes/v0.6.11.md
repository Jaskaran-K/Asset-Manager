## What's new in Asset Manager 0.6.11

### 3D models are read safely, apart from the app
3D models are now read by a helper program of the app's own: for the viewer, the grid's thumbnails and the face counts.

- **No more stutter.** While a big model was read, the window used to freeze for a third of a second at a time. Now it stays smooth (on a 302 MB model: the worst pause went from 367 ms to 23 ms).
- **Less memory.** The app's own memory stays low while models are read (on that model: 180 MB instead of 777 MB).
- **A bad file can't take the app down.** If a model crashes its reader, needs more memory than there is, or takes too long, only the helper stops. The card says **"Couldn't read"**, and the file isn't read again until it changes. The viewer always tries again.
- **Within limits.** The helpers together may use up to half your computer's memory, and they close with the app.
- **Face counts for bigger files.** `poly:` searches now cover models up to 1 GB (it was 60 MB). The smallest are read first, so a big one never holds up the rest.

### Values, guides and Mirror Board in the Artbook
For studying references. Nothing in the pictures themselves changes.

- **Values:** right-click > Images > Selection > **Values** shows images as 2 to 5 flat greys, black to white: the light and shadow shapes without the detail. **Shift+V** (Values Board) does the whole board, with the number of tones under Canvas > Values Board Tones.
- **Guides:** Selection > **Guides** draws **Thirds**, a **4x4** or **8x8 grid**, the **Golden Ratio**, **Centre and Diagonals**, or a **Custom Grid** of any number of columns and rows over the selected images. The lines stay thin at any zoom and show in exported PNGs.
- **Mirror Board:** **Shift+H** shows the whole board flipped left to right, the classic way to spot proportion mistakes. Shift+H again turns it back. Everything still works while it's mirrored.

### Video frames
Right-click a video while it plays (or paused) for **Send Frame to Artbook**, **Copy Frame** or **Save Frame As PNG...**. The frame is at the video's full resolution and named after the video and the time, like "Creating The Head 12:41". Playback carries on.

### Also changed
- New speed tests check that loading, searching and counting stay quick on a 50,000-file library, so a slowdown is caught before a release.
