## What's new in Asset Manager 0.6.14

### AI tools
Two new tools run AI models on your computer; nothing is uploaded. Each downloads the first time you use it, and **Settings > Storage** removes it. They use the graphics card (any DirectX 12 card: NVIDIA, AMD or Intel), or the processor if there isn't one. They run in a helper program of their own, so a driver crash can't take the app with it.

### AI Search
Click the **AI button** in the search box and describe what you're looking for, such as "creature skull side view" or "brick wall". Files whose pictures match come first, whatever they're called. The download is 410 MB.

- **Filters still apply:** tags, filters and the sidebar still narrow the results, and saved searches keep AI Search on.
- **What it reads:** pictures, pictures inside archives, and the thumbnails of models, videos and PDFs. Your files are read once in the background, and again only when they change.
- **More Like This** (right-click a file) shows files with the same kind of content, closest first: other skulls, other brick walls. Find Similar is for copies of the same picture.
- **Tag suggestions:** the tag window suggests the tags of the most similar files you've already tagged. Click one to tick it. They get better the more you tag.

### Remove Background
Right-click pictures to make a copy of each with its background removed. The copy is saved beside the original as `name_cutout.png`, with transparency.

- **In the Artbook,** use **Images > Remove Background...** to see each cut-out next to the original before it goes on the board. One Ctrl+Z undoes it, and **Revert to Original** works any time.
- **What it's for:** pictures with a subject, such as a render, a photo of an object or a character. A full scene has none.
- **Two models,** each downloaded the first time you choose it. The choice is remembered.
  - **BiRefNet** (520 MB): the best on most pictures, about 1 second a picture on a recent graphics card.
  - **BEN2** (250 MB): faster (0.3 seconds), and a second try that often gets right what BiRefNet misses.
- **Your pictures are never changed.** A button in the window moves the new cut-outs to the Recycle Bin.
