## What's new in Asset Manager 0.6.5

### HDRI lighting in the 3D viewer
The HDRIs in your library are now lighting choices, listed in the **Lighting** menu under the presets. **Browse for an HDRI...** takes any other panorama, and right-clicking an HDR image in the grid offers **Use as Viewer Lighting**.

- **Only real panoramas are listed.** Colour 2:1 images are found by reading their headers, so renders, bakes and renderers' thumbnails stay out, inside archives too. One HDRI saved as both `.hdr` and `.exr` is listed once.
- **Real light from the image.** Every pixel is read, so a sun that holds most of the light in a few pixels isn't missed. Up to three of its brightest sources become directional lights, and the rest gives the soft light. This avoids the false dark bands such a sun otherwise leaves on a model. A 4K HDRI takes under a second the first time, in the background, and going back to one you've used is instant.
- **It stays in the scene.** Orbit and the reflections move as they should. **Shift+drag** turns the HDRI, and **Auto Rotate** spins the model inside a still environment. Each HDRI starts with its strongest light at the front right, where it shows the form.
- **Exposure and Backdrop.** Exposure is set automatically, and a slider goes three stops either way. **Backdrop** (**B**) shows the HDRI behind the model.
- **The materials respond to it** (Normals stays as it is, for checking normals), and the 3D view of an alpha gets it too. Your HDRI, exposure and backdrop are remembered. The presets look as they did, and so do thumbnails.

There are no cast shadows or ambient occlusion: one part of a model doesn't shade another.

### Metal with a roughness slider
**Metallic** no longer looks like chrome. A **Roughness** slider shows when it's picked, from a mirror at 0 to a soft sheen at 100, under HDRIs and presets alike. It starts at a satin 50 and is remembered.

### UV view
Press **U** (or the **UV** button) to see a model's UV layout, with quads and n-gons as they are in the file. UVs are only read when you ask, so opening a model costs nothing extra.

- **UDIM tiles** in use are lit and numbered, and listed in the details panel as **UV Tiles**. Squares outside UDIM show their position.
- **Big layouts stay smooth**: they're drawn on the graphics card (4 million edges took under 4 ms a frame on an RTX 4070). Drag to pan, scroll to zoom toward the pointer, **R** to fit.
- **UV sets:** a file with more than one gets a picker.
- OBJ and binary FBX show true polygons. glTF and others show triangles. Text-format FBX files say their UVs can't be read.

### Model stats
The viewer now says what a model is made of, instead of the triangles it's drawn with.

- A 7-million-quad sculpt reads **7,079,846 quads** in the details, not 14 million "tris". The panel lists **Polygons, Quads, Triangles, N-gons** and **Triangles When Drawn**. The badge says quads or tris when a model is all one kind, and polygons when it's mixed.
- **Vertices** counts the points in the file, not the extra ones the viewer makes to draw hard edges.
- **Dimensions** have units: cm from an FBX (following its unit setting), metres for glTF, and plain "units" for formats that don't say.
- `poly:` in the search still counts triangles, as before.

### Also changed
- The 3D viewer's toolbar has two rows: **UV**, **Auto Rotate** and **Reset View** moved to the second, which also holds the sliders.
- The viewer's details panel scrolls, so long lists and tags are no longer cut off in a small window.
