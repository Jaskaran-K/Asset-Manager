## What's new in Asset Manager 0.3.5

### 🪟 Slide-out frosted sidebar
- The sidebar slides in and out with the new **menu button** at the top left, or **Ctrl+B**. Drag its right edge to make it wider or narrower; the app remembers the size.
- **Docked or floating:** with the **pin** button on, it stays docked beside the grid. Unpin it and it floats over the grid on frosted glass, with your thumbnails blurred behind it. It slides away after you pick a category, press **Esc** or click beside it.
- **Glass Frost** slider (**Settings > Appearance**): from clear glass to fully frosted. Previews live while you drag.

### 🎨 Backgrounds and icons
- **Background textures** (**Settings > Appearance**): Film Grain, Dots, Grid, Diagonal Lines, Hexagons, Carbon Fiber and Topographic, as a faint pattern behind the grid and dialogs. Each one is tinted to suit the theme, and the grid's texture stays still while the cards scroll over it.
- **New two-tone icons** throughout the app replace the old emoji symbols. They take their colors from the theme, change with it, and stay sharp on high-resolution screens.

### ✨ Smoother browsing
- **Smooth scrolling:** each mouse-wheel notch glides instead of jumping, everywhere in the app. Touchpads work as before.
- **Categories slide open** instead of popping in when you expand them.
- **Library split button:** **Library** and **Add to Library** are now one button. Click it to go back to your library; the arrow adds the folder you're browsing (or removes it, if it's already in the library). It's highlighted while your library is showing.

### 🛠️ Fixes
- No more stray highlighted square to the left of hovered or selected items in the sidebar and the Settings menu. Hovered items now light up as you move over them.

### ⚠️ Good to know
- All the new motion follows **Settings > Appearance > Animations**: turn it off (or let it follow Windows) and sliding, gliding and fading become instant.
- Unsigned app: Windows SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info > Run anyway**.
