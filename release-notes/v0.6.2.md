## What's new in Asset Manager 0.6.2

### Look inside ZIP, RAR and 7z archives
Your archives are now part of the library, without unpacking them.

- **Each archive is a card** with the best picture inside (one named like *preview* or *cover* first), or an archive box listing what's inside ("MP4, SRT"). Its badge says how many files it holds ("ZIP · 124"), and its tooltip lists them by type.
- **Double-click an archive** to look inside it like a folder. **Back** or **Backspace** returns to where you were, with the archive selected. Search and the sidebar work inside it.
- **The files inside show among the rest of your library,** marked with a small box, so search finds them. Turn it off with **Include Files Inside Archives** in the Library button's menu.
- **Images inside get real thumbnails,** read straight from the archive: a screen of 40 in about 1 to 1.5 seconds. Their resolution is searchable (`res:`), and tags, favourites and ratings work on them like on any file.
- **Course videos play from inside archives.** Opening a video, model, PDF or anything over 100 MB unpacks it first, with a progress bar and Cancel: a 240 MB lesson in about 1.5 seconds. Unpacked copies are kept (up to 20 GB, the oldest go first), so opening it again is instant.
- **Extract To...** (right-click an archive) unpacks it into a folder named after it; **Extract...** saves one file. Nothing is replaced without asking, and names that could escape the folder are skipped.
- **Drag a file out of an archive** into another app: it's unpacked first (up to 50 MB; bigger ones go through Extract).
- **Deflate64 zips** are read too. Many zip readers can't open them, Python's included.
- **RAR and 7z archives** work the same way, through 7-Zip (free, and found by itself; or set it in **Settings > Archives**). A multi-part RAR is one card. Without 7-Zip, their cards say "Install 7-Zip to look inside".
- A damaged archive says so on its card instead of stopping the scan. Password-protected files say so too.

On a library with 215 zips and 32 RARs (547 GB), scanning stays quick: each archive's contents are listed once and remembered, so a full rescan of 8,636 files, 5,434 of them inside archives, takes about a quarter of a second.

Your file types and sidebar categories get **.zip**, **.rar**, **.7z** and an **Archives** category once, when you first start this version. If you remove them afterwards, they stay removed.
