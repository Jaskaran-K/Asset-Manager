## What's new in Asset Manager 0.7.0

### Artbook: plan with cards
The Artbook now holds plans as well as pictures: to-dos, columns, tables, links and lines, side by side with your references. Pages from earlier versions open as before.

**Cards**
- **The tool rail:** on the left of the board. Click a card for one in the middle, or drag it where you want it. Double-clicking empty space offers the same, plus **Link...**.
- **Headings and notes:** a heading is a big title with an accent line (**Shift+T**). In a note, `#`, `-`, `1.` or `[]` then Space starts a heading, a list or a checklist.
- **To-dos** (**X**): tick a line and it sinks to the bottom. A count and a bar show how far along it is.
- **Columns** (**Shift+C**): drag cards and pictures in to stack them. The arrow folds a column to its title and count.
- **Tables** (**Shift+B**): text, numbers with a total, and status chips you can rename and recolour. Tab and Enter move between cells.
- **Link, file and page cards:**
  - Paste a web address for a card with its title, picture and the site's icon.
  - Drop in a Maya scene or any other file for a card that opens it, in Maya first.
  - Drag a page from the Pages list for a card that opens that page.
  - Each card comes **Big** or **Compact**.
- **Card colours:** right-click > **Card Colour** puts a strip down a card's edge, colours a column's outline, or a heading's line.

**Lines**
- **Joining cards:** hover a card and drag a dot on its side to another card, or use the **Line** tool (**Shift+L**).
- **Styles:** curved, straight or elbow, with or without arrows, in any colour, solid or dashed, with a label.
- **Points on lines:** double-click a line to put a point on it, then drag the point to route the line round things. Other lines can join a point, and deleting it joins the line back up.

**Working on a page**
- **Snapping:** dragged things snap into line with their neighbours and to equal gaps. Hold **Alt** to drag freely. The arrow keys nudge the selection.
- **Duplicate, copy and move:** **Ctrl+D** duplicates. **Ctrl+C** / **Ctrl+V** copy cards to any page, with their pictures and comments. Right-click > **Move to Page** or **Copy to Page** sends them elsewhere.
- **Comments** (**M**): on a card or picture, or as a numbered pin anywhere. Threads can be ticked done.
- **The tray** (**U**): **Unsorted** holds what your browser sent. **Trash** keeps what was deleted from the page for 30 days.
- **Mouse controls:** the board shows them in light words at the bottom right. They brighten on hover, and right-click > Canvas > Navigation Hints hides them.

**Pages**
- **A header on every page:** its name, a line about it, and what's on it. Double-click the header to rename the page. For pages made before this version, use right-click > Pages > **Add Headers to Every Page...**.
- **Find** (**Ctrl+F**): searches this page or every page. The Pages search finds pages by what's on them too.
- **Go to a page** (**Ctrl+P**): type a few letters of its name. Your recent pages come first.
- **Templates:** start a new page from Project board, Mood board, Character sheet or Review notes, or from a page you've saved as a template.
- **Export:** the new **Export** button saves a PNG, a JPG or a PDF. In a PDF, link and file cards can be clicked. **Ctrl+Shift+C** copies the page as a picture.

### Browser extension
- **Unsorted:** right-click a picture > **Add to Artbook** > **Current Page** or **Unsorted**.
- **Pages as links:** right-click a page or a link > **Add This Page as a Link**. Its title, picture and icon are read in your browser, so sites that turn the app away, such as ArtStation, still get a preview.
- **Updating:** after installing this version, click **Reload** on the extension in your browser's extensions page.

### Changes
- **Ctrl+C on the board** copies the cards themselves, so **Ctrl+V** pastes cards, not text. Other apps still get the text or the picture.
- **Ctrl+E** exports a PNG with your last export settings. **Ctrl+Shift+E** opens the export options.
- **Double-clicking a line** now puts a point on it. To type a label, double-click the label, or select the line and press **Enter**.
