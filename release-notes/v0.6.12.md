## What's new in Asset Manager 0.6.12

### Search by colour
Type `color:red` to find pictures with red on 15% of them or more. The names are red, orange, yellow, green, cyan, blue, purple, pink, brown, skin, black, white and grey.

- **Any colour:** `color:#c04020` finds colours close to that one, and `-color:grey` leaves a colour out.
- **The palette button** in the search box lists the names, or opens a colour picker (it can take a colour from the screen too). The search follows the colour as you pick it.
- **Normal maps are left out** of colour names, so `color:blue` finds blue pictures, not every normal map.
- **A picture's main colours** show in its tooltip. Right-click > **Search by Its Colours** finds pictures with one of them.

### Find Similar
Right-click a picture > **Find Similar** shows it and its look-alikes from the whole library, closest first: resized or re-saved copies, and very close versions.

- On a real library it found 100% of half-size copies, 97% of low-quality JPEG re-saves and 90% of slightly cropped copies. Unrelated pictures matched 0.06% of the time.
- It shows as a "Similar to ..." chip in the search box: remove it, combine it with other searches, or save it as a collection.
- **Tools > Find Look-alikes...** groups look-alike pictures, the biggest file first, so extra copies can go to the Recycle Bin. Check the groups first: a turntable's frames, or an alpha and its JPG preview, look alike too.

**How it works:** each picture is looked at once, in the background, and again only when it changes. The first time takes a while on a big library (about 9 minutes for 8,500 pictures, half of them inside archives). Searches fill in as it goes, and the window stays smooth.

### Folder rules
**Settings > Folder Rules**, or right-click a file > **Folder Rule...**: "files added to WIP get the tags WIP and Kitbash".

- New files, and files copied or moved into the folder, get the tags once. That includes files added while the app was closed: they're tagged at the next scan.
- A tag you take off a file stays off.
- Making a rule can also tag the files already in the folder.
- Rules work in every profile, and follow tags being renamed or deleted.

### Fixed
- **Pictures inside big zips no longer pile up in memory.** Zips packed with Deflate64 (Windows makes these for big zips) kept every picture read from them in memory for good: thumbnails, and now colours. 2,000 pictures took 2.5 GB. They now take nothing once read.
- Closing the app now waits for background work, so it can't crash on the way out.
