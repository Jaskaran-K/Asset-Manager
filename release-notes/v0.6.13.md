## What's new in Asset Manager 0.6.13

### Texture sets
Maps that belong together (`Rock_BaseColor`, `Rock_Normal`, `Rock_Roughness`...) now show as one card with the label "SET". UDIM tiles of one set make one card too.

- **Library > Group Texture Sets** turns it on or off.
- Tagging, favorites, ratings, Send to Artbook and dragging a set card apply to all its maps. Drag one into Substance Painter's shelf or Blender's node editor.
- A search that finds any of a set's maps shows the set.
- The names of most programs and stores are understood: Substance, Megascans, Poly Haven, Unreal and more. Words like `_OpenGL`, `_DirectX`, `_Raw` and `_sRGB` are understood too.

### The material preview
Double-click a set (or right-click > **Preview Material**) to see it as a material on a sphere, cube or plane, under the viewer's lights or your HDRIs.

- **The maps it uses:** colour, normal, roughness or gloss, metallic, AO, emissive, opacity, packed ORM maps, and height as real displacement.
- **Flip Green** is for DirectX normal maps, and is guessed from the names.
- **Untick a map to leave it out**, or change what kind of map it is.
- **When a set has two maps of one kind:** an OpenGL normal map is used over a DirectX one, a Displacement map over a Bump map, and an EXR over a PNG or JPG of the same name.
- **The Adjust sliders:** colour, normal, roughness, metal, AO, emissive and height. Each shows its value, and a double-click resets it. They change the preview only.
- **Height** is the map's depth as a share of its width, so bricks look equally deep on every shape and at any tiling.

### Send to Blender / Maya as Material
Right-click a set, or use the buttons in the material window, to make it a material in the Blender or Maya that's open. The material goes on the selected objects there, as one undo step.

- **Blender:** a Principled BSDF.
- **Maya:** an Arnold **aiStandardSurface**, with an aiNormalMap, Raw colour space for data maps, and displacement on the shading group. Turn on the mesh's Arnold subdivision to see the displacement. **Settings > Apps** can make it a Maya standardSurface instead.
- **It needs the helper** from **Settings > Apps**. Installed helpers update themselves; restart Blender or Maya once afterwards.
- Maps inside archives are unpacked first.

### Rename
Select files and press **F2** (or right-click > **Rename...**) to rename them from a pattern, with a preview first.

- **Names are made from** the old name, the set's name, the kind of map, a number, the folder and the UDIM tile. Find and replace, and changing the letter case, work too.
- **Map words can be rewritten** in Substance's style (`_BaseColor`, `_Normal`) or short (`_BC`, `_N`, `_R`).
- **Names that would clash, can't be used, or are too long are flagged first.** Swapping two names works.
- Tags, ratings and favorites stay with the renamed files.
- **Ctrl+Z** undoes it.

### Convert and resize
Right-click pictures > **Convert and Resize...** to make copies as PNG, JPG, TGA or TIF.

- **Sizes:** 512 to 4K, a size of your own, or the nearest power of two.
- **Where:** a subfolder, or a folder you pick.
- **16-bit height maps and masks stay 16-bit** in PNG and TIF.
- **Your files are never changed.** A button in the window moves the new copies to the Recycle Bin.
