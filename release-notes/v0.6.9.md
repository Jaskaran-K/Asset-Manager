## What's new in Asset Manager 0.6.9

### Upscale images in the Artbook
Small references look soft when you zoom them up on a board. **Upscale** makes them 2 or 4 times bigger with [Real-ESRGAN](https://github.com/xinntao/Real-ESRGAN), so they stay sharp at the size you look at them.

- **Where:** select images, then right-click > Images > **Upscale...**.
- **Two kinds of picture.** **Photo** suits photos, renders, sculpts and paintings. **Illustration** suits line art, manga and anime: clean lines and flat tones.
- **Compare before you use it.** Drag the line across each picture to see the original and the upscaled one side by side. The mouse wheel zooms in, and dragging moves around. Untick any you don't want, then **Use Upscaled Pictures**.
- **Nothing moves.** Upscaled images keep their place and size on the board, and the whole change is one Ctrl+Z.
- **The original is always kept.** Images > **Revert to Original** brings it back, even after the page is closed and reopened. Your files are never changed: the bigger picture is kept in the page.
- **Quick.** A second or three per picture on a graphics card. Real-ESRGAN is free, downloads once (45 MB) and runs on NVIDIA, AMD or Intel graphics.

**It invents believable detail rather than recovering the real thing.** Most references come out well, but small text and fine material detail (a sword's wrap, a fabric's weave) can come out wrong, which is why comparing comes first. Results are at most 6000 pixels on the long side, and images already close to that size can't be upscaled.

### Notes: backgrounds pasted with text
Text pasted from a web page could bring a coloured background behind whole paragraphs that couldn't be removed.

- Highlight > **No Highlight** now removes every background behind the text, including ones pasted before. Picking a highlight colour replaces it.
- The highlight button shows a pasted background's colour.
- **Ctrl+V** while typing in a note keeps the text's formatting but leaves out page backgrounds, and **Ctrl+Shift+V** pastes plain text in the note's own style.
