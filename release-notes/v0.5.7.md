## What's new in Asset Manager 0.5.7

### A better 3D viewer
- **Big models open quickly:** the viewer opens at once and the model loads in the background. A 300 MB sculpt with 14 million triangles used to freeze the app for nearly a minute; it now takes a few seconds, with about half the memory.
- **Colour:** pick the model's colour with the Artbook's colour picker. The model changes as you drag.
- **Materials:** Matte, Clay, Phong, Glossy, Metallic, Toon, and Normals (shows the surface directions, to check a model's normals).
- **Lighting:** Studio, Soft, Dramatic, Top and Headlight.
- **Turn the model or the lights:** drag turns the model and Shift+drag turns the lights. **Turn Lights** (**L**) makes a plain drag turn the lights. A small dial shows where the main light is.
- Colour, material and lighting are remembered for the next model.

### Thumbnails of 3D models
- Models now get a rendered picture in the grid. It's made once, in the background, and kept with the other thumbnails.
- Models over 150 MB get theirs the first time you open them in the viewer, so scrolling past them stays light.
- A picture next to the model (`model.png`) is still used when there is one. Settings > Appearance > **Pictures of 3D models** turns them off.

### Themes
- **New theme: Neon Cyan.**
- **Theme Studio:** a separate window for making your own themes and background textures. Open it from the Start menu (Asset Manager Theme Studio).
  - Every colour of a theme, with a live preview: the main window, a dialog, the frosted sidebar over an Artbook page, and every icon.
  - Gradients for window, panel and grid colours.
  - Backgrounds from patterns, or from an image of your own made seamless, tinted with any colour.
  - Themes are saved as `.amtheme` files. The guide is in Settings > Help.

### Fixes
- Fixed random crashes in the PDF viewer, in Settings while switching themes, and in the main window. They came from scroll bars being freed while still in use.
- Drawing long strokes on a PDF no longer slows down as the stroke grows.
- Opening the 3D viewer for the first time no longer makes the main window disappear and come back.

### Good to know
- Unsigned app: Windows SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info > Run anyway**.
